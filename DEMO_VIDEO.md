## ⚡ Core Pitch in 3 Bullet Points

* **⭐ One-Line Problem:**  
  *"Businesses lose significant revenue when customer payments fail, but recovering that revenue safely at scale is difficult."*

* **⭐ One-Line Solution:**  
  *"RecoverAI uses Google Gemini AI to recommend smart recovery actions and a deterministic policy engine to safely recover measurable revenue."*

* **⭐ Strongest Tagline:**  
  > **RecoverAI — AI recommends. Policy decides. Executor acts.**

### **Part 3: 5 Key Real-World Scenarios (1:30 – 3:00)**
* **On Screen:** Navigate to **Recovery Cases** in the sidebar. Show the **Featured Demo Scenarios** top banner and click into **`CASE-1001`** and **`CASE-1002`**.
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"On the Recovery Cases view, we highlight 5 deterministic presentation scenarios:*
  >
  > 1. ***Scenario 1 (`CASE-1001`) — High-Probability Recovery:***  
  >    *A ₹2,999 UPI payment failed due to a transient timeout. AI diagnoses the glitch and recommends an instant WhatsApp payment link. The Policy Engine evaluates 5 safety rules, approves it, and upon execution, 100% of the funds are recovered!*
  >
  > 2. ***Scenario 2 (`CASE-1002`) — The Policy Safety Guardrail:***  
  >    *A ₹1,499 card payment with 3 prior declines. An ordinary AI bot might blindly recommend retrying. But our **Policy Engine intercepts it**: Rule 1 blocks automated retries after 3 attempts and diverts it to human ops to protect merchant credit standing.*
  >
  > 3. ***Scenario 3 (`CASE-1003`) — High-Value Merchant Guard:***  
  >    *A ₹75,000 transaction. Even if the failure is retryable, Policy Rule 2 enforces our ₹50,000 safety cap, escalating the case to a finance specialist.*
  >
  > 4. ***Scenario 4 & 5 (`CASE-1004` & `CASE-1005`) — Stopping Rules & AI Circuit Breaker:***  
  >    *Consecutive execution failures automatically halt retry loops, and if the external LLM goes offline, our circuit breaker seamlessly activates deterministic rule sets with zero downtime.*"
